# Page Topology: Google Account Chooser

## 1. Main Card (Centered)
- **Visual Order:** Center of the page.
- **Layout:** Flex row on desktop, 2 columns. Left column has headers, right column has input and actions. Dark mode theme.

### Left Column
- **Top header:** Google Logo and "Sign in with Google"
- **Title (H1):** "Sign in"
- **Subtitle:** "to continue to WordPress"

### Right Column
- **Input field:** "Email or phone" (floating label style).
- **Link:** "Forgot email?"
- **Text:** "Before using this app, you can review WordPress's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service."
- **Actions (Footer of right column):** 
  - "Create account" (link style button)
  - "Next" (solid button, pill shape)

## 2. Page Footer
- **Layout:** Flex row, justify-between.
- **Left:** Language selector dropdown ("English (United States)").
- **Right:** Links "Help", "Privacy", "Terms".
